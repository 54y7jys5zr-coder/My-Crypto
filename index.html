Holdings Outside Kraken

Coins in a private wallet that never went through Kraken — for example a gift. Saved on this device, included in backups, and counted in your totals.

    If you later move these coins to Kraken, delete the entry here so they aren't counted twice.

    Watch-Only Addresses

    Add a public address of your own wallet to check its balance against what's recorded here. BTC, ETH, LTC and DOGE (native coins). The blockchain explorer sees which address is looked up — never enter a seed phrase or private key.

      Alerts

      Coin alerts are set from a coin's detail sheet. Checked on every price refresh; notifies while the app is open if allowed (iPhone: Home Screen app, iOS 16.4+).

        Backup & Restore

        Save your imported data, holdings outside Kraken, alerts and settings to a file, or restore it on any device. Your API key is not included.

        Erase removes everything this app stores on this device: imported data, holdings outside Kraken, alerts, settings, your API key, stored CSVs and the downloaded sentiment model. It can't be undone.

        CoinGecko API Key

        About

        Strict-FIFO cost basis in EUR. All Holdings counts everything you own, including gifts & staking rewards. Invested Only counts just the coins you paid for.

        Prices from CoinGecko, Kraken (live) and DefiLlama; exchange rates from the ECB via Frankfurter; wallet balances from mempool.space, Blockscout and BlockCypher. This is an analysis tool — not tax or investment advice.
